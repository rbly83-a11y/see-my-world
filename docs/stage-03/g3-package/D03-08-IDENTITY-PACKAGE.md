## 1. The identity system the boards present

The seven boards present a complete, consistent **SEE MY WORLD** product identity. It is specified here exactly as depicted.

### 1.1 The mark

An eight-pointed **compass-star** in gold line work, with a small central circle/diamond — a navigator's star. It appears:

- as the hero emblem centered above the wordmark on the welcome screen (Board 00), and again at the foot of that screen;
- at the left of the wordmark in desktop headers (Board 00 Atlas; Passes 04–09 mastheads);
- beside panel titles and in the mobile status area (Pass 05 phone frames);
- as the closing lockup mark in board footers (Board 00 bottom-right; Pass 09 Creator Status medallion variant);
- as a worn emblem on the depicted guide figure's cloak (Passes 06, 09) and as the center of the Life Constellation (Pass 07) — identity integrated into illustration, not only into chrome.

### 1.2 Wordmark and tagline

- **Wordmark:** "SEE MY WORLD" in letterspaced Playfair Display capitals, gold-to-pearl rendering as depicted. Pass 04–09 mastheads carry a "™" glyph after the wordmark as a design element; no trademark registration is authorized or claimed by this package (Charter §5).
- **Tagline (as printed):** "Your World · Your Lens · Your Perspective" — printed in the Board 00 welcome screen as "YOUR WORLD • YOUR LENS • YOUR PERSPECTIVE" and in every Pass 04–09 masthead as "YOUR WORLD • YOUR LENS • YOUR PERSPECTIVE".
- **Footer lockup (Board 00):** compass-star above "SEE MY WORLD" above "YOUR WORLD • YOUR LENS • YOUR PERSPECTIVE".

### 1.3 Color identity

The identity palette is the D03-07 palette (Midnight, Ocean, Amethyst, Gold, Teal, Pearl) with the compass-star consistently rendered in **Gold** and hero imagery in the Sunset/Cosmic gradient family. Token values and the Board 00 / Pass 04 discrepancy are recorded in D03-07 §§1, 10 — not repeated here.

### 1.4 Imagery identity

A consistent illustrated world: a castle city above waterfalls at sunset, island territories joined by light, a great violet tree on a cliff (Passes 05–09 left rails), aurora skies. The Founder-persona "Bobby Bly — World Owner" (Passes 04–09 mastheads; "World Curator" on Pass 06; "World Creator" on Pass 09) is depicted example content, not an identity rule.

## 5. The chartered-scope gap — stated plainly

**The charter defines D03-08 as the "PARAVEX conceptual identity package"** (Charter §7). Under A-51 §5, PARAVEX is a candidate visual and marketing identity only: the chartered G3 deliverable is the package presenting the dual-cat manga-inspired emblem, the psychedelic cracking-globe motif, the wordmark, typography, color system, and app-icon concepts, clearly marked conceptual, for evaluation through usability and recognition testing — with no canonical rename, no repository/package/database/API rename, no trademark registration, no domain purchase, and no public launch claim.

**The boards deliver a different (and, on its own terms, complete) thing:** the SEE MY WORLD product identity system specified in §§1–3 above.

Therefore:

- **D03-08 is PARTIALLY COVERED by this package.** Delivered: a full product identity system (mark, wordmark, tagline, usage, voice) at E1. **Outstanding: the PARAVEX conceptual identity package as chartered** — no PARAVEX emblem, motif, wordmark, or icon concepts exist in the filed design material, and none are invented here.
- This is a gap against the charter's deliverable definition, **not** a naming violation: presenting SEE MY WORLD as the product identity is what the governing record requires of product surfaces (D03-04 rows 26–27). The Founder's gate decision must dispose of the gap explicitly — e.g. accept the SEE MY WORLD identity system as the G3 identity deliverable and re-scope or defer the PARAVEX candidate package, or direct that the PARAVEX package be produced. This package recommends neither; it records the gap.
- Until the Founder decides, PARAVEX remains exactly what A-51 made it: a candidate identity under evaluation, unauthorized for adoption, rename, registration, or launch use.
