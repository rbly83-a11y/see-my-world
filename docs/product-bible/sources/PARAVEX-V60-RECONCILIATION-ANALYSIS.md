## 1. Identity of Edition 60, and the Edition 59 → Edition 60 delta

### 1.1 What Edition 60 is — measured, not as self-described

Edition 60 is a **two-stratum compilation**:

- **pp. 1–562 — "Volume A": the complete v58 document, byte-for-byte.** Verified: all 334 pages of v58's integration volume are textually identical to E60 pp. 1–334, and all 228 pages of the appended Edition 35 source are identical to E60 pp. 335–562 (334/334 and 228/228 pages identical after whitespace normalization). Edition 60's own document control concedes this: "Volume A is preserved without alteration in the combined PDF" (E60 p. 563); "This edition preserves the entire user-supplied 562-page v58 PDF as Volume A without changing its pages" (E60 p. 564).
- **pp. 563–582 — "Volume B" (20 pages, ~7,500 words): the only new material.** It has two parts: an "Edition 60.0" addendum proper (pp. 563–570, §§1–25) and an annex titled "Prior Consolidated Review Edition" (pp. 571–582, §§1–23 plus appendices), a synthesis compiled from an inspection of the GitHub repository and local "Pass 139–183" artifacts.

**Consequence that governs everything below:** because Volume A is v58 unaltered, *every* v58 conflict that lives in Volume A persists in Edition 60 exactly as the A-58 analysis recorded it. All repair in Edition 60 is located in Volume B, and Volume B nowhere expressly supersedes Volume A — on the contrary, the annex recommends the file be labeled "Consolidated Review Edition, not Edition 60 or a final superseding Bible, until Edition 59 and Upgrade v5 have been inspected and reconciled… and the Founder records explicit acceptance" (E60 p. 582). Each conflict grade in §2 therefore reads: *Volume A position (unchanged) vs Volume B position (the repair, where one exists) vs net status.*

### 1.2 What Edition 59 was

Edition 59 (602 pp) was itself a three-part compilation, verified by the same page-identity method: pp. 1–3 a new front-matter integration register (document control, a "Targeted Supersession Register," a CHRONOS CHESS domain specification, and editorial findings); pp. 4–565 the complete v58 document verbatim (562 pp, offset +3); pp. 566–602 the 37-page *Unified Project Upgrade Edition 5.0* ("ATLAS + OMEGA | PROPOSED EDITION 3.0"), containing the OMEGA work-package families (OMEGA-105–142 specified; OMEGA-79–104 expressly "unverified/missing… must not be fabricated," E59 p. 2) and the CHRONOS LIVE passes 11–13.

Words (uniform `pdftotext -layout` counts): 183,422 → 177,512 = **−5,910 words** (the larger ~11,500-word figure obtained by comparing A-61's ~189,022 against E60's 177,512 mixes two extractors and should not be used). No volume or register of the v58 corpus was added, removed, or reordered — the corpus is identical, at a different offset. The only new register is Volume B's own decision register (E60 pp. 570, 580–581).

### 1.4 Edition 60 is a sibling of Edition 59, not its successor

This is stated by the document itself, twice: the annex's source-completeness notice records that "the previously referenced PARAVEX Integrated Edition 59, Upgrade v5, and v58 social-universe PDF were *not directly retrievable for inspection in this session*" and that "before declaring this the final authoritative successor to Edition 59, reconcile those three documents paragraph-by-paragraph and obtain Founder sign-off" (E60 p. 571); §23 repeats that the three PDFs were "previously discussed but not inspectable in this session" and that the edition "intentionally does not fabricate their detailed contents" (E60 p. 581). Volume B was compiled from the repository, the charter, and local Pass 139–183 artifacts — not from Edition 59.

**What was therefore lost in the 59 → 60 step** (present in 59, absent from 60): Edition 59's front-matter status line, which was accurate as reported — "Current reported status: Stage 02 frozen; Stage 03 A-51 authorized; G1 bounded accepted; G2 not yet assigned" (E59 p. 1); its Targeted Supersession Register (the only attempt in either compilation to state, item by item, which proposed text supersedes which); and the entire Upgrade v5 stratum, including the CHRONOS Session Coordinator specification, SPECTATOR SYNC 2.0, the OMEGA-105–142 work packages, and the CREATOR DNA / STREAMFORGE / STREAMBRIDGE / PORTALPASS / SPOTLIGHT growth apparatus. CHRONOS survives in Edition 60 only as a re-derived proposal summary (E60 pp. 567, 576), without the v5 engineering detail. Anyone treating Edition 60 as "59 plus fixes" would silently discard that material; the correct description is: **60 = 58 reissued verbatim + a repository-grounded reconciliation addendum, compiled in ignorance of 59.**

## 3. New material and new conflicts in Edition 60

### 3.1 NEW CONFLICT — C-13: the reported G3 authorization and the Pass 160–183 development line. Grade: factual/governance. **New in Edition 60; the most serious item in this analysis.**

Volume B asserts, as fact, a Founder authorization that the project record does not contain:

- "Stage boundaries: Stage 02 accepted and frozen. **G3 design development was separately Founder-authorized**, while G2 disposition remains unresolved and G3 formal gate acceptance remains pending" (E60 p. 572).
- "Founder authorization to pursue G3 design development does not itself constitute acceptance of G2 or formal G3 gate signoff" (E60 p. 569).
- Decision register: "G3 formal acceptance — Pending; **development authorized**" (E60 p. 580).

On that premise, Volume B reports an entire development line executed under it: "Pass 172 audited visual gaps. Pass 173 delivered an original SVG Living Atlas. Pass 174 added Studio and Lens demonstrations… Pass 178 replaced iframe composition with a single native DOM… its local Chromium verification recorded 39/39 checks… through the reported Pass 183" (E60 p. 568), tabulated at length in the annex's "G3 Development Record — Passes 160–183" (E60 p. 577), including claimed partial coverage of charter deliverables ("Pass 177 · G3 acceptance-readiness dossier · **D03-07/08/09 partial**; gate not accepted") and a proposed repository integration ("apps/experience-g3/… **NOT present in repo**," E60 p. 578 — proposed only, and expressly unapproved).

Against the accepted record: the charter's sequence is G2 (D03-05/D03-06) before G3 (D03-07/D03-08/D03-09) (A-51, gate table); its execution restriction bars work from beginning until tasks are explicitly assigned (A-51: "No implementation work shall begin until G1 tasks have been explicitly assigned," applied gate-by-gate in A-52/A-57); G1 was accepted with G2 expressly requiring separate assignment (A-57); and A-69 — the Founder's ruling commissioning this very analysis — records "No G2 assignment is made by this entry; the Stage 03 gate state is unchanged." **No register entry authorizes G3 design development, with or without a G2 disposition.** The claim is also anomalous on its face: a G3 authorization that leaves "G2 disposition unresolved" inverts the charter's gate order, and Volume B itself states the gates are "distinct" and sequential in effect (E60 p. 569).

Under the project's standing evidence discipline (A-45), an authorization reported by an external session is *reported* authorization, not verified authorization; the register is primary. Two readings are possible, and this analysis does not choose between them: (a) the Founder gave some direction in the other session that its compiler characterized as a G3 authorization — in which case the register needs a Founder-confirmed entry recording exactly what was authorized, because the Pass 160–183 work's status depends on it; or (b) no such authorization exists, in which case Edition 60 misstates the gate state on the one point where it claims to have repaired the record, and the Pass 160–183 line is unassigned work product. Either way: **the G3 prototype artifacts are not project evidence, the "development authorized" premise must not be used in any G2/G3 planning document, and the D03-07/08/09 "partial" claims have no standing** until the Founder rules. The mitigating facts, recorded for fairness: the annex labels its pass counts "historical reported results, not independently rerun" (E60 p. 577), states the prototypes use "synthetic data, no live permissions, no durable storage and no production services" (E60 p. 568), and holds the repository integration as "a proposal, not implicit authorization" (E60 p. 580).
